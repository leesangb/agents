# Restate

From the current conversation, explain in your own words what the user wants to achieve and what problem they are trying to solve. Distinguish the goal from a proposed solution, preserve constraints that affect the decision, and make material assumptions explicit. Keep it concise and in the user's language, focused on checking shared understanding rather than proposing an implementation.
