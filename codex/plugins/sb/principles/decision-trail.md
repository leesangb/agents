# Decision Trail

Use when long-running, unattended, or multi-session work has material choices, pivots, or abandoned approaches that the final diff cannot explain. Ordinary small tasks need no separate trail.

Keep one trail in the existing task record or a local scratch artifact. At each consequential decision or checkpoint record the choice, its reason, an evidence pointer with the relevant artifact/version, and the observed result or unresolved condition. Include an alternative only when rejecting it changed the direction. Record checkpoints as verified, failed, or inconclusive according to actual evidence; inconclusive is not a pass.

Record decisions as they occur. If reconstructing a missing entry later, label it reconstructed and cite the source; do not invent the rationale. Correct an earlier entry with a superseding explanation while retaining the evidence that led to the pivot. The trail does not replace the approved plan or authorize scope changes.

On pickup, compare the prior record with current source, working-tree state, and available verification evidence. Keep historical verification tied to its artifact; do not present it as a fresh check. Account for reversions and abandoned approaches that constrain the next step. Use [Recall](../skills/recall/SKILL.md) when the caller requests reconstruction across sessions rather than already supplying enough context.

Before handing back, resolve material evidence pointers and reconcile the claims against observed results. Report consequential gaps or uncertainty in the normal result. No fixed file format, extra reviewer, commit, or publication is required; keep temporary trails out of commits unless explicitly requested.
